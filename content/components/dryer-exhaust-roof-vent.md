## Description
Termination cap for a clothes dryer duct through the roof.

## Function
Exhausts lint-laden air. Different requirements from a bath vent.

## Replacement notes
Must have a backdraft damper. Must not have a screen — lint clogs it and that's a fire issue. This is the key distinction from a bath vent, because people swap these interchangeably and shouldn't. A bath vent cap is not an acceptable dryer termination if it's screened.

## Measurement notes
Duct diameter (4"), damper type, roof pitch.
