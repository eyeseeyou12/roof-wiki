## Description
An opening left in the deck where equipment used to be — a removed cooler, a dead attic fan, an old flue, a relocated vent. Swamp coolers are a dry-climate appliance, extremely common in Denver, Utah, Phoenix, and West Texas, and much less so in Central Texas humidity — regional prevalence of the swamp-cooler-specific case needs local judgment, but the general abandoned-penetration case is common everywhere.

## Function
None, and that's the point. Someone finds a mystery patch, a rusted curb, or a hump under the shingles and needs to know what it was and what to do.

## Replacement notes
Options are usually: patch the deck with new sheathing and shingle over, or reuse the opening for something else. A removed cooler opening commonly becomes a skylight, solar tube, or attic fan, since the hole is already there and framed.

## Measurement notes
Whether the deck under it is sound — rot around an abandoned penetration is common and is the actual issue. Opening dimensions. Whether anything is still connected below — wiring, water line, ductwork.
