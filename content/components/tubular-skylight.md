## Description
Reflective tube running from a roof dome down to a ceiling diffuser.

## Function
Daylight into interior spaces with no line of sight to the sky — hallways, baths, closets. Cheaper and less invasive than a skylight.

## Replacement notes
Match diameter and covering. The roof-side dome is often replaceable on its own when the acrylic clouds or cracks, which is a much smaller repair than the whole assembly.

## Measurement notes
Tube diameter — 10" and 14" are standard and fit between 16"-on-center framing; 22" exists for larger rooms. Roof covering — flashing is specific to shingle vs. tile vs. metal, and some models ship without a flashing kit at all. Rigid vs. flexible tube. Whether there's a solar night light or venting option.

## Failure modes
Acrylic dome degrades and yellows in UV; seal at the flashing; condensation in the tube.
