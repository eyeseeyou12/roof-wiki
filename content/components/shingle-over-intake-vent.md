## Description
Intake installed in the roof plane a short distance up from the eave, shingled over so it's nearly invisible.

## Function
Intake where soffit, fascia, and drip edge options are all blocked — closed eaves, cathedral ceilings, additions, complicated eave details.

## Replacement notes
The problem-solver option when nothing else works. More expensive and more invasive than soffit.

## Measurement notes
Roof covering, distance from the eave, deck cut requirements.
