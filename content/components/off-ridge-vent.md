## Description
A long, thin static vent placed over a cut slot near but not at the ridge. Functionally between a box vent and a ridge vent. People confuse these with box vents constantly — the distinguishing observation is shape: long and thin rather than square.

## Disambiguation
Shares the alias "low profile vent" with static-roof-vent (box vent) — the same manufacturer term normalizes to both. Shape is the distinguishing observation: long and thin here, square on a box vent.

## Function
Exhaust where the ridge itself can't be used — short ridgelines, hips, or where the ridge is interrupted.

## Replacement notes
Match the slot length and profile.
