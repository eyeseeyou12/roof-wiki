## Description
The small holes — antenna and satellite mounts, HVAC refrigerant linesets, condensate lines, conduit, mini-split penetrations. This is a catch-all entry and may want to split once real search behavior shows how people look for these.

## Function
Individually minor, collectively a common leak source, and frequently flashed badly or not at all — just sealant.

## Replacement notes
This is the main use case for retrofit and split flashings (see retrofit-pipe-boot) — you can't disconnect a live lineset or a mast to slide a boot down it. Cut-to-fit EPDM or silicone boots cover a wide OD range and are the usual answer.

## Measurement notes
OD of whatever is passing through. Whether it's still in service (abandoned dish mounts are everywhere). Whether the penetration is at an angle, which most standard boots don't handle. Mini-split installs put small penetrations in roofs and walls that weren't there twenty years ago — a real 20-year change and one you'll see more of.
