## Description
Rigid or foam channel stapled between rafters at the eave that holds an air gap open between the insulation and the roof deck. Not visible from outside, which is why this is the one intake component people never think about.

## Function
Keeps blown insulation from burying the soffit vent. When someone says "my soffit vents don't work," the answer is very often a missing or crushed baffle — the connection this wiki should make from a soffit vent question to this entry.

## Measurement notes
Rafter spacing (16" or 24" on center), material (cardboard, foam, plastic), depth of insulation.
