## Description
Two-piece or slit flashing that wraps around a pipe already in place and locks closed.

## Function
Flash or re-flash a penetration where the pipe can't be lifted — repairs, storm work, masts, HVAC stacks.

## Replacement notes
Also the substitute for a standard boot when access is the constraint. Worth noting the competing repair: a universal rubber collar that slips over an existing intact metal or lead base, which is a smaller job than replacing anything.

## Measurement notes
Pipe OD (these are usually cut-to-fit within a range), roof covering, whether the base tucks under shingles.

## Disambiguation
"Split boot" is used for two different things depending on who's talking: a boot whose collar has split (a failure condition — see pipe-boot's failure modes), and this two-piece product that wraps around an existing pipe without pulling it. Someone searching "split boot" could mean either.
