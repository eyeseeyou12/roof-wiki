## Description
Cap on top of a flue that keeps rain out while letting exhaust escape.

## Function
Rain and debris exclusion, and on some designs, draft protection.

## Replacement notes
Same wall type, same diameter, rated for the appliance category. Same-brand as the pipe is the safe answer for B-vent.

## Measurement notes
Flue diameter. Single-wall vs. B-vent — different cap types, not interchangeable. Whether the appliance is a Category I natural-draft unit or high-efficiency (high-efficiency units usually vent PVC out a sidewall, not a roof flue).
