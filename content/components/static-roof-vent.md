## Description
Passive exhaust vent over a cut opening near the ridge. No moving parts. Still the default and cheapest option, but ridge vent has taken a lot of share on new installs and re-roofs (20-year note).

## Function
Lets hot, moist attic air out. Pairs with soffit intake — exhaust without intake doesn't do much.

## Replacement notes
Any static vent with the same throat size and a compatible profile. Colors and brands are interchangeable. This is one of the easier swaps on a roof.

## Measurement notes
Throat/hole size — this is the number that matters, not the outside dimensions. 12" is common. Profile: slant back vs. pan/dome style. Material: galvanized, aluminum, or plastic. Color.

## Disambiguation
"Louver vent" is also used for gable vent, a different component in the vertical gable wall rather than the roof plane. See gable-vent. Also shares "low profile vent" with off-ridge-vent — people confuse the two constantly; shape distinguishes them, square here vs. long and thin on an off-ridge vent.
