## Description
Hooded or louvered cap where a bath fan duct terminates through the roof.

## Function
Dumps humid air outside the envelope. Terminating into the attic instead of through the roof is a common defect and a moisture problem.

## Replacement notes
Match duct size. Screened is fine here — bath vents may be screened. A dryer termination can serve a bath fan, but not the reverse.

## Measurement notes
Duct diameter (4" typical), whether there's a damper, hood style.
