## Description
Flanged base that laces into the shingles, with a collar that seals around a plumbing vent pipe where it exits the roof.

## Function
Sewer gas vents to the outside; the boot keeps water from running down the outside of the pipe into the deck.

## Replacement notes
Same nominal pipe size, base compatible with the roof covering. Material is an upgrade decision, not a compatibility one — lead and silicone outlast rubber. If the pipe can't be lifted or disconnected, you need a retrofit or split type rather than a standard boot.

## Measurement notes
Pipe diameter. Sizes are nominal, not the measurement you take off the outside of the pipe. Most residential PVC vents are 2", 3", or 4". Collar material: rubber/neoprene, silicone, or lead. Base material: aluminum, galvanized, or plastic. Roof pitch — some bases are pitch-specific.

## Failure modes
Rubber collar cracks and splits from UV, typically well before the shingles are done. Chalking, curling, rust at the flange, exposed nail heads. Sealant over a split collar is a delay, not a repair.
