## Description
Wind-driven exhaust vent with a spinning head on a base flange. Falling out of favor, partly on appearance, but still very common on existing Central Texas roofs (20-year note).

## Function
Same job as a static vent, with wind-assisted draw.

## Replacement notes
Like-for-like, or — the useful part — there are static replacement heads made to drop onto an existing turbine base and throat size. That's the common move when a turbine is noisy, seized, or the homeowner doesn't want another one. Match the throat size.

## Measurement notes
Throat size (12" and 14" are the common residential sizes), base pitch, material.

## Failure modes
Bearings seize, gets noisy, head wobbles or gets blown off in high wind, which leaves an open hole.
