## Description
Attic exhaust fan with an integrated or separate photovoltaic panel. No wiring to the house.

## Function
Same as a PAV, without the electrical run or the power draw.

## Replacement notes
Common upgrade path from a dead electric PAV, since it reuses the hole and drops the wiring problem. Match throat size.

## Measurement notes
Wattage — 20W through 60W are common residential sizes, and it drives the CFM and the attic square footage it covers. Whether the panel is integrated on the housing or remote-mounted. Panel tilt adjustability, which matters on a roof plane facing the wrong way. Whether it has a thermostat, humidistat, or both. Whether there's a hybrid/night adapter.

## Failure modes
Panel degradation, controller failure, and the same intake-balance critique as a PAV. Hail is a live concern in this territory — some units advertise hail resistance and Texas windstorm documentation specifically, a real regional differentiator.
