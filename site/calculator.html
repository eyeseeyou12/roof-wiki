CSV format and example

Use one row per roof face or pitch group. Area columns can be area_sqft, area_squares, or area_m2. With a plain area column, add a unit column or choose units during review. Pitch can be 6/12 or separate pitch_rise and pitch_run columns. Attic is optional; set it to separate spaces that are not connected. Do not include waste.

label,area_sqft,pitch,attic
Main front,1300,5/12,Main attic
Main rear,900,6/12,Main attic
Garage,400,4/12,Garage attic

Separate spaces are sized independently. No airflow is shared between them.

How this was calculated

Net free area (NFA) is the open ventilation area, not the outside size of a vent. Roof-based attic area is an estimate; verify overhangs and excluded areas.
