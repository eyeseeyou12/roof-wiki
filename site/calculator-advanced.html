Attic ventilation calculator

Estimates required net free area from IRC R806.2, then translates it into quantities of generic vent types (or a specific brand's real products). This is a starting point to verify, not a spec — go measure.

Upload roof measurements

Read a PDF report or CSV, review the measurements, then add them to the calculator. Files are read on this device and are not uploaded or saved.

PDF: selectable-text pitch/area tables; scanned diagrams need manual entry. ESX: export a roof-measurement PDF or CSV first; native ESX decoding is not supported yet.

CSV format and example

Use one row per roof face or pitch group. Area columns can be area_sqft, area_squares, or area_m2. With a plain area column, add a unit column or choose units during review. Pitch can be 6/12 or separate pitch_rise and pitch_run columns. Attic is optional; set it to separate spaces that are not connected. Do not include waste.
